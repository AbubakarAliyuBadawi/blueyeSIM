## How the injection times were found

- **SN**: no disturbance on `/scenario/events` or `/blueye/virtual/*_command`; whole mission used.
- **SC**: first disturbance at 168.1 s (sonar Near (simulated hazard, /scenario/events)), so data end at 158.1 s. Disturbances in order: 168.1 s sonar Near (simulated hazard, /scenario/events); 178.6 s sonar Critical (simulated hazard, /scenario/events); 333.7 s usbl Weak (injected, /scenario/events); 343.6 s sonar Near (simulated hazard, /scenario/events) ….
  - 2.4 s: usbl Strong (command) is a nominal state, not a disturbance.
- **FN**: no disturbance on `/scenario/events` or `/blueye/virtual/*_command`; whole mission used.
- **FC**: first disturbance at 46.8 s (sonar Critical (injected, /blueye/virtual/sonar_command and /scenario/events)), so data end at 36.8 s. Disturbances in order: 46.8 s sonar Critical (injected, /blueye/virtual/sonar_command and /scenario/events); 101.4 s usbl Weak (injected, /blueye/virtual/usbl_command and /scenario/events); 112.1 s sonar Near (injected, /blueye/virtual/sonar_command and /scenario/events); 124.1 s sonar Critical (injected, /blueye/virtual/sonar_command and /scenario/events) ….
- In SC the rocks are static objects with no injection time. `field_twin_events` logs the sonar readings they cause on `/scenario/events` (`source: simulated`), and the first of them (sonar Near at rock A) comes before the first scripted USBL change. In FC the HUD's injection commands on `/blueye/virtual/*_command` and `/scenario/events` give the same times.

## Not measured in some runs

Reported per run in the CSV, left out of the pooled frequencies and the comparison:

- USBLStrength in field: no USBL fitted: virtual_sensors holds Strong unless a state is injected on the HUD.
- SonarRange in field: no sonar fitted: virtual_sensors holds Clear unless a state is injected on the HUD.
- Current in field: not measured: virtual_sensors publishes 0 m/s.
- USBLStrength in SC: held Strong by field_twin_events from the start (the field run's virtual USBL).
- ArUcoVisibility in FN: entered only from marker detections kept from an earlier attempt ('acquired' gating).
- Fatigue and Stress come from the synthetic operator model (linear rise over 15 min) in every run, so their frequencies describe that model, not an operator.
